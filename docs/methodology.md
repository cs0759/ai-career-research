# Research Methodology

This website is an evolving research project, not a live labor-market database.

## How to research a role

1. Collect current, publicly accessible job postings from employers.
2. Record the company, exact title, location, posting date, and source URL.
3. Extract responsibilities, required skills, preferred skills, and seniority.
4. Compare several postings before describing common patterns.
5. Distinguish direct evidence from interpretation; date any market claims.
6. Revisit sources regularly because titles and requirements change.

## Suggested primary sources

- [OpenAI Careers](https://openai.com/careers/)
- [Anthropic Careers](https://www.anthropic.com/careers)
- [Google Careers](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/)
- [Microsoft Careers](https://careers.microsoft.com/)

These links are starting points, **not** citations for the specific role summaries on this site. Specific claims should eventually link to individual job postings or other direct evidence.

## Contributions

Open an issue or pull request with a source URL, date accessed, and proposed correction. Avoid reproducing full copyrighted job descriptions.
