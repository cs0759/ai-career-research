## What these postings suggest

1. **Full-stack and API skills remain useful:** Several FDE and applied AI postings require integrations, backend work, debugging and customer-facing delivery.
2. **Evaluation is central:** Employers repeatedly mention tests, observability, latency, guardrails and reliable production behavior.
3. **Customer collaboration matters:** FDE work often combines software development with discovery, deployment and stakeholder communication.
4. **Agent engineering is broader than prompts:** Employers describe tool use, orchestration, state, recovery and security.
5. **Seniority varies greatly:** An internship, a 0–2 year junior role and a 6+ year specialized FDE position may share a title family.

## Additional market context

An [August 2026 independent Agentic AI Jobs Index](https://www.getreadyforagents.com/jobs/report/2026-08/) reported 412 forward-deployed-engineer postings and 123 agent-engineer postings in its tracked sample.
