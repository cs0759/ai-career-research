# US Labor-Market Evidence

*Source review: November 5, 2026.*

The US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) measures established occupations, **not** emerging company-specific titles such as Forward Deployed Engineer, AI Agent Engineer or LLM Engineer. Use these charts as context, not as title-specific forecasts.

## Wage context: median annual pay in May 2025

| Established occupation | Median US annual pay |
| --- | ---: |
| Data scientists | $120,230 |
| Software developers | $135,980 |
| All occupations | $50,980 |

Sources: [BLS Data Scientists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/math/data-scientists.htm), [BLS Software Developers](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/software-developers.htm). These are occupation-wide medians, **not entry-level salaries or salaries for emerging AI job titles**.

## What students should conclude

- Software development fundamentals are relevant to many emerging AI application roles.
- Job titles alone are insufficient to infer experience level, salary, or technical duties.
- Read employer postings and compare requirements with an evidence-based skills roadmap.
- US-remote listings may be limited to particular states or require specific work authorization.
