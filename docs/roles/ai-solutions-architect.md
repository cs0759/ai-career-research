# AI Solutions Architect

## What does this role do?

AI solutions architects plan how AI capabilities fit into a company's existing infrastructure, data, security requirements, and business processes.

## Common responsibilities

- Translate business needs into system designs.
- Select models, platforms, and integration patterns.
- Evaluate scalability, security, governance, and cost.
- Guide teams through proof-of-concept and deployment decisions.

## Skills to investigate

Cloud architecture, APIs, data engineering, security, AI system design, communication, and technical documentation.

## Research questions

How much hands-on implementation is expected? What cloud and governance certifications are useful?
